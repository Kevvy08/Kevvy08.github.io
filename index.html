About Honor Volt

Honor Volt is an early-stage renewable energy initiative focused on delivering reliable and practical domestic solar PV solutions. The venture is built on a strong engineering foundation, hands-on field experience, and a commitment to honest system sizing and workmanship.

Honor Volt is led by Kudzai Kelvin Bandera, an electronic engineering student and solar systems technician with practical exposure to residential, commercial, and large-scale solar installations.

Our Services

  • 5 kVA Domestic Solar System Installations
  • Solar PV system upgrades and expansions
  • Inverter configuration and commissioning
  • System inspection, maintenance, and fault diagnosis
  • Basic energy usage assessment and system recommendations

All installations are approached with a strong emphasis on safety, correct component sizing, and long-term system reliability.
