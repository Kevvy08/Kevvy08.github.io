About Honor Volt

Honor Volt is an early-stage renewable energy initiative providing reliable domestic solar PV solutions. The focus is on honest system sizing, quality workmanship, and long-term reliability.

The initiative is operated by Kudzai Kelvin Bandera, an electronic engineering student and solar systems technician with hands-on experience in residential, commercial, and large-scale solar installations.

Our Services

  • 5 kVA Domestic Solar PV System Installations
  • Solar system upgrades and expansions
  • Inverter configuration and commissioning
  • System inspection, maintenance, and fault diagnosis
  • Basic energy usage assessment and system recommendations

All services are delivered with strict attention to safety standards and correct component selection.
